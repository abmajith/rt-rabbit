Bus Handshake & Read/Write Protocols
    Zero-Wait-State Acknowledge: 
    The core asserts wb_ack_o exactly 1 clock cycle following a valid cycle qualification:
        $$\text{bus\_write\_en} = \text{wb\_cyc\_i} \cdot \text{wb\_stb\_i} \cdot \text{wb\_we\_i} \cdot \overline{\text{wb\_ack\_o}}$$
        $$\text{bus\_read\_en} = \text{wb\_cyc\_i} \cdot \text{wb\_stb\_i} \cdot \overline{\text{wb\_we\_i}} \cdot \overline{\text{wb\_ack\_o}}$$

    Bus Deadlock Prevention: 
    Because wb_ack_o clears unconditionally on the clock cycle following an assertion, the peripheral cannot stall or hang the system crossbar interconnect.

    Default Slicing Safety & Unmapped Reads: 
    If unmapped addresses (2'b10 or 2'b11) are requested during a Read command, the data bus safely squashes output leakage by returning 32'd0 explicitly.

    CPU Hardware Override:
    The tracking accumulator supports active runtime presets. Software can clear index position tracking or write an explicit offset directly into reg_position at any cycle, taking immediate priority over raw encoder ticks.


Hardware Tracking & Synchronization Engine
The core features a parallelized 3-stage shift pipeline to guard against metastability and coordinate sample timing perfectly:

    Metastability Immunity Link: 
    Raw pin inputs pass through a multi-stage flip-flop buffer before evaluation:
        $$\text{shift\_a}_n = \{\text{shift\_a}_{n-1}[1:0], \, \text{enc\_a\_pad\_i}\}$$
        $$\text{shift\_b}_n = \{\text{shift\_b}_{n-1}[1:0], \, \text{enc\_b\_pad\_i}\}$$

    Phase Edge Alignment Criteria: 
    Direction decoding is locked to Channel A transitions to prevent jitter count bloat:
        $$\text{a\_rose} = (\text{shift\_a}[2:1] == 2'\text{b01})$$
        $$\text{a\_fell} = (\text{shift\_a}[2:1] == 2'\text{b10})$$

    Directional Sign Resolution:
    When a valid transition is confirmed on Channel A, Channel B's synchronized midpoint state resolves the step vector:
        $$\text{Count Step} = \begin{cases} +1 & \text{if } (\text{a\_rose} \cdot \overline{\text{shift\_b}[1]}) + (\text{a\_fell} \cdot \text{shift\_b}[1]) \\ -1 & \text{if } (\text{a\_rose} \cdot \text{shift\_b}[1]) + (\text{a\_fell} \cdot \overline{\text{shift\_b}[1]}) \end{cases}$$
